MyHFGuard — Notification and Dashboard Advice Guide
Updated 6 September 2026

REMAINING PHONE NOTIFICATIONS

1. Medication: 12:00 PM and 9:00 PM (Malaysia time).
   Only medicines assigned to that period appear. A noon-only medicine never
   appears in the night reminder, and a night-only medicine never appears at noon.
   Medicines marked both appear in both. No reminder is sent for an empty list.
   Old 10 AM entries are treated as noon; old 10 PM entries as 9 PM.
   Profile medicine entries override matching older medicine-table entries.
   Explicit medication schedules take precedence over the medicine table's timing.
   Inactive table medicines are excluded. A profile line without a time defaults
   to noon, matching the profile's existing convention; unknown explicit times
   are not scheduled automatically.
   The alarm targets the exact time; Android battery restrictions can delay it.
   A WorkManager fallback operates during the scheduled hour. Delayed jobs outside
   that hour are skipped so a noon reminder cannot arrive at night.
   Reminders are deduplicated per patient/date/period. Tapping opens Dashboard.

2. Immediate blood pressure/pulse alerts after a successful Self-Check BP save.
   RED: SYS >=180 or <80 mmHg; DIA >=120 or <50 mmHg;
        pulse <50 or >150 bpm.
   YELLOW (when no red rule matches): SYS >=140; DIA >=90;
        pulse <60 or >100 bpm.
   Tapping opens Dashboard. Yellow alerts can repeat after another saved reading.

3. Immediate symptom alerts after a successful Self-Check symptom save.
   Each of the five symptoms has a score of 0–5; severe means >=4.
   RED: at least three severe symptoms OR total score >=20/25.
   YELLOW: at least one severe symptom OR total score >=12/25, without red.
   Tapping opens Dashboard. Yellow alerts can repeat after another saved entry.

4. Background health alerts: checked approximately every hour.
   Runs for the signed-in patient, using today's available records.
   RED conditions:
   - SYS >=180 or <80; DIA >=120 or <50 mmHg.
   - Pulse or smart-band heart rate <50 or >150 bpm.
   - SpO2 <90%.
   - At least three symptoms scored >=4 today.
   - At least two severe symptoms on each of three consecutive days.
   - Water intake > prescribed limit +200 ml.
   - Weight gain >=3 kg since the previous reading, OR >=3 kg compared with
     the most recent reading at least two days earlier.
   - Today's weight >=5 kg above the patient's dry-weight baseline.
   YELLOW conditions (if no red condition is present):
   - SYS 121–179; DIA 80–119 mmHg.
   - Pulse or heart rate <60 or >100 bpm; SpO2 <95%.
   - At least one severe symptom, or at least two symptoms scored >=3.
   - Water intake above the prescribed limit by up to 200 ml.
   - Salt score >=7/9, or a stored red salt status.
   - Weight gain >=1.5 kg since the previous reading, OR >=2 kg compared with
     the most recent reading at least two days earlier.
   - Today's weight >=3 kg above the patient's dry-weight baseline.
   Only available measurements are evaluated. Missing inputs do not generate
   phone reminders. Background yellow alerts repeat when the recorded reasons
   change, rather than repeating the identical last alert every hour.

5. Dashboard red alert: when Dashboard evaluates a red condition and its
   alert state/reasons change, including when Dashboard is opened.
   Uses the existing dashboard AlertEngine:
   SYS >=180 or <80; DIA >=120 or <50; pulse/heart rate <50 or >120;
   SpO2 <90%; recent weight gain >=3 kg; or weight >=5 kg over baseline.
   This is a phone alert; the coloured dashboard status itself is also retained.

EXISTING ALERT DIFFERENCES TO BE AWARE OF
The existing immediate/background notifications share a one-red-alert-per-patient-
per-day gate. The existing Dashboard red notification uses its own path and can
repeat. Dashboard pulse/heart-rate red thresholds use >120, while immediate and
background notifications use >150. Immediate BP yellow starts at 140/90; the
background check also includes 121–139 SYS and 80–89 DIA. These existing clinical
thresholds were preserved in this enhancement.
Salt's red graph/status category starts at 7/9, but the existing background salt
phone notification is YELLOW. A red graph label does not change that policy.
Health workers use the phone's calendar day; medication/appointment timing uses
Malaysia time. Android notification permission, channel settings, connectivity,
and background execution affect whether/when a phone notification is delivered.

REMOVED PHONE NOTIFICATIONS
- Appointment reminders (appointments remain available on their page).
- Missing daily weight, BP, symptoms, water, salt or band refresh reminders.
- Steps/4 PM prompts.
- Saturday education prompts.
Previously scheduled jobs are cancelled or finish without sending a reminder.
Old non-health/non-medication entries are hidden from notification history.

DASHBOARD CHECKLIST AND APPOINTMENTS
- Medicine tick boxes appear only from 12:00:00–12:59:59 and
  21:00:00–21:59:59 Malaysia time, when at least one medicine is due.
- Tick only after taking that medicine. Unticking is supported.
- Ticks are saved locally on this device, separately for patient/date/period/
  medicine/dose. Noon ticks do not tick the night dose. A new date starts unticked.
  These ticks are not uploaded to the administrator or synced between devices.
- Appointment cards appear from 30 minutes before the scheduled appointment
  through its scheduled start. They are hidden at other times, with no empty card.
  Both appointment-table and appointment-type reminder records are supported.
- The dashboard clock updates while open; health data refreshes on resume/day change.

CLICKABLE MISSING-ENTRY ADVICE
These entries appear together whenever today's data is absent, with no phone alert.
- Missing weight -> Self-Check.
- Missing BP -> Self-Check.
- Missing symptoms -> Self-Check.
- Missing water -> Water & Salt.
- Missing salt -> Water & Salt.
- No recorded/synced steps today -> Exercise.
The item disappears after the record is saved and Dashboard refreshes.

GRAPHS
Self-Check now has four graphs: weight, SYS+DIA, pulse, and symptom alert level.
- Weight: dashed patient dry-weight baseline, labelled in kg.
- SYS+DIA: two reading lines plus separate labelled patient SYS/DIA baselines.
- Pulse: separate graph with the patient's heart-rate baseline in bpm.
- Symptoms: green/yellow/red bands plot the recorded alert level rather than
  assuming that a total symptom score alone describes risk.
  Red = any existing immediate or background red symptom condition.
  Yellow = any existing immediate or background yellow symptom condition.
  Green = none of those conditions. Recorded zero is valid; no entry is not zero.
- Water: dashed prescribed limit follows each record's saved limit in ml.
- Salt: labelled threshold at 7/9, where the existing salt status turns red.
No patient baseline is invented when the profile cannot be loaded.
