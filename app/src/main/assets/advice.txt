Daily monitoring
Daily weight not recorded
“⚖️ Please record your weight today. Checking it at the same time each morning helps identify possible fluid buildup.”
Weight recorded normally
“✅ Your weight has been recorded. Continue monitoring it daily so changes can be noticed early.”
Sudden weight increase
“⚠️ Your weight has increased quickly. This may be caused by fluid buildup. Please check for swelling or breathing difficulty and contact your healthcare team.”
Blood pressure not recorded
“🩺 Remember to check your blood pressure today. Sit quietly for five minutes before taking the reading.”
Blood pressure reading advice
“Keep your feet flat, support your arm at heart level, and avoid talking while measuring your blood pressure.”
Blood pressure higher than usual
“⚠️ Your blood pressure is higher than your usual reading. Rest for a few minutes and measure it again.”
Blood pressure very high
“🚨 Your blood pressure is dangerously high. Please seek urgent medical advice, especially if you have chest pain, breathing difficulty, weakness, or severe headache.”
Blood pressure low with symptoms
“Your blood pressure is low. Sit or lie down safely. Please contact your healthcare provider if you feel dizzy, weak, confused, or faint.”
Heart rate unusual
“💓 Your heart rate is outside your usual range. Rest and check it again. Seek medical advice if it remains unusual or you feel unwell.”
Low oxygen level
“⚠️ Your oxygen level is low. Sit upright, rest, and check the reading again. Seek urgent help if you are struggling to breathe.”

Daily tracking of weight, symptoms, swelling and breathing changes can help identify worsening heart failure earlier.

Symptoms and feelings
Mild shortness of breath
“Please sit upright and rest. Avoid strenuous activity until your breathing returns to normal.”
Severe shortness of breath
“🚨 Severe or sudden breathing difficulty requires immediate medical attention. Please contact emergency services now.”
Breathlessness when lying down
“Breathing difficulty when lying flat may be important. Sit upright and contact your healthcare provider as soon as possible.”
Chest pain
“🚨 Chest pain or pressure may be an emergency. Stop your activity and seek immediate medical assistance.”
Swollen feet or ankles
“🦶 New or worsening swelling may indicate fluid buildup. Record your weight and contact your healthcare team if it continues.”
Feeling dizzy
“Sit or lie down to prevent falling. Stand up slowly and avoid walking alone until the dizziness improves.”
Feeling very tired
“Your body may need more rest today. Divide activities into smaller tasks and take breaks between them.”
Vomited once
“I’m sorry you’re feeling unwell. Rest and take small sips only if this follows your prescribed fluid plan. Monitor whether the vomiting happens again.”
Repeated vomiting
“Repeated vomiting can affect your fluids and medication. Please contact your healthcare provider, especially if you cannot keep medicine or food down.”
Persistent cough
“A new or worsening cough, especially at night or when lying down, should be monitored. Contact your healthcare provider if it continues.”

Breathlessness, sudden weight gain, swelling, fatigue, dizziness, nausea and persistent coughing can be associated with worsening heart failure.

Medication, diet and water
Medication reminder
“💊 It is time for your medication. Take it exactly as prescribed by your healthcare provider.”
Missed medication
“Do not take an extra dose unless your doctor or pharmacist has instructed you to. Check your medication instructions or contact them for advice.”
User wants to stop medication
“Please do not stop or change your medication by yourself, even if you feel better. Discuss any concerns or side effects with your healthcare provider.”
Medication running low
“Your medication may be running low. Arrange a refill early so you do not miss any doses.”
High-salt meal recorded
“🧂 This meal may contain a lot of salt. Choose lower-sodium foods for your next meal to help reduce fluid retention.”
Healthy meal recorded
“🥗 Good choice! A balanced, lower-salt meal supports better heart-health management.”
Water intake approaching limit
“💧 You are approaching your daily fluid target. Remember that drinks, soup, ice and watery desserts may also count.”
User asks how much water to drink
“Your fluid limit should follow your healthcare provider’s instructions. I should not increase or reduce it without your personalised care plan.”

Heart-failure treatment commonly includes prescribed medication and individual guidance about sodium and fluid intake; limits should be personalised by the healthcare team.

Exercise and follow-up
Steps below target
“🚶 You are below your step target today. Try a gentle walk or light movement if you feel well and your healthcare provider allows it.”
Symptoms during exercise
“Stop exercising and rest if you experience chest pain, severe breathlessness, dizziness, weakness, or an unusual heartbeat. Seek medical help if symptoms do not improve.”
