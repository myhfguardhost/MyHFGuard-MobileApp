MyHFGuard AI Nurse - Messenger-style floating bubble update
Date: 2026-08-21

Changed files:
1. app/src/main/java/com/vitalink/app/ui/screens/FloatingAiNurse.kt
2. app/src/main/java/com/vitalink/app/navigation/NavHost.kt

New behavior:
- Drag the AI Nurse icon freely around the available screen area.
- Release it and the icon snaps to the nearest left/right edge.
- Drag it slightly past the left or right edge to tuck/hide about half of the bubble, similar to a Messenger chat head.
- The partially hidden bubble remains tappable. Tap it to restore and open the AI Nurse.
- The last side, vertical position and tucked/visible state are remembered across app screens.
- The bubble stays above the bottom navigation area.
- The full AI Nurse card is kept inside the screen even if the bubble is near a corner.
- Existing Gemini, speech recognition, TTS and four-language behavior were not intentionally changed.

Build verification note:
The source was checked structurally in the provided environment. A full Gradle compile could not run because the Gradle wrapper attempted to download Gradle 8.7 from services.gradle.org, but this execution environment has no direct network access.

Build fix (2026-08-21):
- Added missing Jetpack Compose import:
  import androidx.compose.foundation.layout.offset
- This resolves the compileDebugKotlin error in FloatingAiNurse.kt where Modifier.offset { IntOffset(...) } was reported as an unresolved reference.
- The dagger.fastInit / dagger.hilt warning shown by kapt is only a warning and is unrelated to this failure.
