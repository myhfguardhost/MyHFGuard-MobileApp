MyHFGuard Custom Notification Sound Integration
Date: 2026-08-21

Audio files supplied in notification sound.zip were converted from MP4 (AAC + video)
to audio-only MP3 files and placed in app/src/main/res/raw/.

Sound mapping
-------------
appointment.mp4                  -> Appointment reminders
blood pressure.mp4               -> Blood-pressure reminders and BP-related alerts
education notification.mp4       -> Saturday heart-failure education reminder
medicine.mp4                     -> 12 PM / 9 PM medication reminders
refresh mi band data.mp4         -> 9 PM Mi Fitness/Mi Band refresh reminder when it is the only missing action
remember to log all data.mp4     -> 9 PM reminder when multiple daily items are missing; fallback health-alert sound
salt.mp4                         -> Salt reminder / salt-related alert
symptom.mp4                      -> Symptom reminder / self-check symptom alert
walk more and move around.mp4    -> 4 PM step-target reminder
water.mp4                        -> Water reminder / fluid-intake alert
weight.mp4                       -> Weight reminder / weight-related alert

Android channel handling
------------------------
Android 8.0+ stores notification sound settings on NotificationChannel objects.
NotificationSoundChannels.kt creates a separate, versioned channel per sound and
importance level. This makes the custom sounds work reliably on Samsung and other
Android phones even when older app versions previously created default-sound channels.

9 PM reminder behavior
----------------------
- Exactly one missing item: play that item's specific sound.
- Only Mi Band refresh missing: play the Mi Band refresh sound.
- Two or more missing items: play the 'remember to log all data' sound once.
This prevents multiple voice clips from playing at the same time.

Important for testing
---------------------
Install this build and allow notification permission. Android users can still manually
change a channel's sound in system Notification Settings; if they do, the system choice
overrides the app default for that channel.
