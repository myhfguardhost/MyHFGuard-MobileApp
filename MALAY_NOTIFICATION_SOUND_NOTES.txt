MyHFGuard - English + Bahasa Melayu Custom Notification Sounds
Updated: 2026-08-21

Behaviour
---------
- App language English: uses the existing English custom notification audio.
- App language Bahasa Melayu: uses the matching Bahasa Melayu custom notification audio.
- App language Mandarin/Tamil: currently falls back to the English audio until dedicated recordings are supplied.
- Android 8+ notification channels include the audio language in the channel ID, so changing the app language also changes the channel sound reliably.
