MyHFGuard Tamil Custom Notification Sounds
==========================================

Added 21 August 2026.

Language behavior:
- English app language -> English custom notification audio
- Bahasa Melayu app language -> Malay custom notification audio
- Tamil app language -> Tamil custom notification audio
- Mandarin app language -> English custom notification audio (fallback until Mandarin recordings are supplied)

Tamil source files were supplied with generic filenames. They were assigned using the established 11-notification order used for the English/BM packs, supported by the clip ordering and duration pattern:
1. video.mp4 -> Medication
2. Video_20260821_224350_035.mp4 -> Appointment
3. Video_20260821_224350_052.mp4 -> Blood pressure
4. Video_20260821_224350_055.mp4 -> Weight
5. Video_20260821_224350_063.mp4 -> Symptom
6. Video_20260821_224350_067.mp4 -> Water
7. Video_20260821_224350_074.mp4 -> Salt
8. Video_20260821_224350_078.mp4 -> Steps/activity
9. Video_20260821_224350_085.mp4 -> Education
10. Video_20260821_224350_087.mp4 -> Mi Fitness refresh
11. Video_20260821_224350_089.mp4 -> Multiple missing daily records / log all data

Android 8+ notification channels cache their sound configuration. CHANNEL_VERSION was increased to v5 so existing installs create fresh language-aware channels and Tamil audio is applied correctly.
