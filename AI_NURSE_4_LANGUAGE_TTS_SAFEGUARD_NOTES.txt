MyHFGuard AI Nurse - 4-language TTS safeguard update (2026-08-20)

Implemented:
1. App-language lock across STT -> Gemini -> TTS.
2. Gemini backend prompts explicitly prevent language drift during Malaysian code-switching.
3. Android TTS tries language-specific locale candidates instead of falling back to English phonemes.
4. If Malay/Mandarin/Tamil voice data is missing, the app keeps the AI response text visible and prompts the device owner to install TTS voice data.
5. TTS installer intent is handled safely; if unavailable, a localized message asks the user to install/update Speech Services by Google.
6. TTS voice availability is checked when the selected app language becomes active.
7. AI Nurse remains hidden on the full My AI chatbot screen.

STT mapping:
English -> en-MY
Bahasa Melayu -> ms-MY
Mandarin -> zh-CN
Tamil -> ta-IN (chosen for broader Android recognizer support; Gemini/TTS output remains Tamil)

Preferred TTS locales:
English -> en-MY, then English fallbacks
Bahasa Melayu -> ms-MY, then ms
Mandarin -> Simplified Chinese / zh-CN
Tamil -> ta-MY, then ta-IN, then ta
